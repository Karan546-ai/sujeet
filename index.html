The Process

Brief, script
and storyboard

Most projects start as a one-line brief. I turn it into a script and storyboard so the story is agreed before anything is generated or cut.

Generate and craft
every frame

Prompt engineering, character consistency and scene continuity across AI tools, with AI voiceover, music and lip sync where the film needs it.

Edit, review
and deliver

Final edit, colour, sound and quality control on every video. With my AI pipeline, a film that used to take 3 days now takes 1.

Frequently
asked questions

01

How fast can you turn a project around?

Often within the week. Most of my projects start as a one-line brief, and the AI production pipeline I built cut a typical film from 3 days to 1.

02

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I've made brand films, AI short films and CGI ad films for clients in India, the UAE and the US, and I handle client communication directly, from the first brief and quote to final delivery.

03

Which AI tools do you use?

Seedance 2.0, Google Flow (Veo 3, Omni Flash), Kling AI, Runway, Higgsfield, Magnific AI and ComfyUI for visuals, with ElevenLabs, Fish Audio and Suno AI for voice and music.

04

Can you lead a team or run post-production?

Yes. At Head Field I lead a team of 4 editors, assign work and deadlines, run quality control on every video and manage the post-production workflow across 14+ business verticals.

05

What kind of role are you looking for?

A Lead Editor role where I can make AI-generated films full time. I'm based in New Delhi, also take freelance projects, and work in Hindi and English.
